# Experience Map

> Status: approved v1.

Each experience carries a scope label: Platform, Fishing domain pack, Catch
America operator, or a combination. The scope label says where the experience
belongs, not who may use it.

## Participant Experiences

**Participant Experience**

Scope: Platform.

For Participants. Help them understand what is happening outdoors, decide what
they may want to do, express Trip Intent, receive useful updates, prepare,
participate in the Activity, contribute outcomes, and receive value in return.

**Fishing Mode**

Scope: Fishing domain pack.

For Participants actively fishing. Aggregate everything relevant to the current
Participant, Location, Trip, target, experts, Field Intelligence, conditions,
rules and access, plan, gear, techniques, and outcomes into a simple on-water
experience.

Boundary: no ecommerce, promotions, campaigns, or selling while actively
fishing.

**Website & Learning Center**

Scope: Platform.

For any Person, whether or not they hold an account or a role, and for
Participants. Turn platform knowledge, Field Intelligence, experts, Locations,
techniques, content, systems, and related products into useful public learning
and discovery experiences that can lead into Trip Intent, following, account
creation, or other relevant actions.

**Community & Social Experience**

Scope: Platform.

For Participants and Contributors. Support the owned community and external
social channels so that community makes the outdoor experience more useful
through relevant information, relationships, contributions, and interaction.
Covers posting, following, commenting, messaging, listening, moderation, Field
Intelligence signals, and user-generated content.

**Commerce Experience**

Scope: Platform and Fishing domain pack.

Primarily for Customers. A Participant who takes a commercial action such as
purchasing or booking may also hold the Customer role. The two roles remain
distinct. Allow straightforward shopping while using Fishing Systems, My Gear
context, expert knowledge, content, and participant context to help customers
prepare appropriately.

Boundary: My Gear informs suggested purchase selection, but the complete
recommended Fishing System remains visible and My Gear never silently removes
components from it.

**Catch Therapy**

Scope: Catch America operator.

For Participants seeking expert help. Let a Participant escalate from AI or
self-service help to an appropriate Expert or Guide with relevant context
carried forward. If a paid or commercial booking occurs, that Person may also
enter a Customer relationship.

## Expert and Provider Experiences

**Expert & Guide Experience**

Scope: Platform, Fishing domain pack, and Catch America operator.

Expert and Guide remain distinct roles. Expert is a contextual expertise role;
Guide is a fishing-domain service-provider role. One shared experience may serve
both because a Person can hold either or both, and the capabilities shown depend
on the roles that Person holds.

Expert-oriented capabilities: contribute knowledge and Field Intelligence with
minimal effort, verify knowledge, correct Max (Catch America operator), create
and distribute content, and earn value.

Guide and provider capabilities: maintain profile and expertise, manage services
and availability, manage schedules and bookings, carry customer context, and
help Participants.

**Provider Organization Experience**

Scope: Platform, with Fishing as the first implementation.

For Provider Organizations such as a guide service. Help the Organization manage
its profile, team, services, availability, bookings, business information,
customer communications, and provider operations, while individual Providers
retain their own Person profiles and roles.

## Internal Workspaces

**Customer & Personalization Workspace**

Scope: Platform.

For customer and personalization teams. Understand who a Person is across
declared, observed, inferred, and contextual information, define and reuse
Audiences, and honor consent and communication preferences.

**Marketing Workspace**

Scope: Platform and Catch America operator.

For marketing teams. Observe opportunities, plan strategy, build campaigns,
define hypotheses, choose approved Audiences, coordinate channels, run
experiments, measure outcomes, and learn. Campaign Preflight Audit (Catch
America operator) is an optional AI-assisted audit performed before campaign
delivery.

**Content Workspace**

Scope: Platform.

For content teams. Decide what should be created, updated, repurposed, or
retired; research from platform knowledge; build briefs; map related products
and systems; coordinate expert review; and keep published content healthy.

**Community & Customer Service Workspace**

Scope: Platform.

For community and customer service teams. Bring together customer and community
interactions across owned and external channels, surface what needs attention,
provide relevant context, assist with grounded responses, and route signals to
the function that owns them.

**Operations Workspace**

Scope: Platform.

For operations and fulfillment teams. Manage exceptions across orders,
fulfillment, inventory, returns, product quality, suppliers, and shipping, using
credible demand signals where relevant.

**Finance Workspace**

Scope: Platform.

For finance teams. Explain how the business is performing economically, and
support financial investigation of why.

**Leadership Workspace**

Scope: Platform.

For organizational leadership. Show what changed, why it matters, what needs a
decision, emerging opportunities and risks, business health, Field Intelligence,
Market & Ecosystem Intelligence, and important system health without becoming a
wall of dashboards.

**Knowledge Operations**

Scope: Platform and Catch America operator.

For teams managing knowledge quality. Work through signals awaiting
verification, conflicting claims, invalidated evidence, stale knowledge, expert
verification, Max corrections (Catch America operator), and source failures.

**Expert / Contributor Operations**

Scope: Platform.

For teams coordinating Experts and Contributors. Manage onboarding and profile
confirmation, contribution and research requests, review and attribution,
rewards and earnings, and participation health.

**Services & Booking Operations**

Scope: Platform.

For teams coordinating Providers and bookings on the operator side. Manage
providers, services, availability, bookings, scheduling, cancellations,
participant context, service outcomes, and provider payout and earnings context.
Provider Organizations manage their own services and team through the Provider
Organization Experience.

**Market & Ecosystem Intelligence**

Scope: Platform.

For teams monitoring external changes and the business functions they inform.
Continuously observe meaningful external changes across competitors, fishing and
outdoor communities, industry, environment, public conversation, and other
relevant sources. Distinguish observation from interpretation, surface trends
and signals, and route them to appropriate business functions.

**Deferred Dedicated Experiences**

Dedicated experiences for Platform Administrator, Retailer / Dealer, and
Organization in Partnership are recognized but not yet defined in Experience
Architecture v1. Their actors remain part of the approved actor model.

## Cross-Experience Principles

- AI may propose, summarize, structure, reason, and assist; it does not own
  truth, business rules, authority, or persistent memory.
- Fresh first-party Field Intelligence is a central source of differentiated
  participant value.
- Field Intelligence may be captured, verified, synthesized, and consumed by
  different experiences and domains. This map does not assign all Field
  Intelligence ownership to one workspace.
- Participant intent is not presence.
- Trip Intent can create a Watch that monitors meaningful change before arrival.
- When appropriate, people communicate naturally by voice, text, photo, or video
  and AI structures the input.
- Before an Activity, the platform may help prepare; during Fishing Mode,
  selling disappears; after the Activity, the platform may help record, share,
  learn, earn rewards, and prepare for future activity.
- Experiences consume shared platform domains; they do not redefine domain
  ownership.
- Experiences and domains are not one-to-one. Not every domain requires its own
  user-facing workspace.
