# North Star

> Status: approved v1.

## Vision

Build an intelligent operating platform that can eventually serve any fishing or
outdoor company.

The platform connects what is happening in the real world, what experts and
participants know, what customers intend to do, what the business is doing, and
what outcomes actually occur. Holding those together in one system is the point
of the platform.

## First Implementation

Catch America is the first operator. Fishing is the first domain pack.

We build for Catch America now. We do not hard-code Catch America into the
platform core.

## Long-Term Platform

The platform may ultimately support commerce, finance, services and booking,
marketing, content and learning, community and social, personalization, rewards
and earnings, subscriptions and memberships, analytics, workflows and
automation, AI, field intelligence, market intelligence, and business
operations.

This is a statement of possible scope, not a commitment to build any of it on
any schedule.

## Strategic Differentiator

Fresh first-party field intelligence is central. It comes from experts and from
people actually in the field, and it is a key source of differentiated, current
real-world intelligence that other platform capabilities can use.

The long-term value is the connected intelligence and the operating system
itself. It is not any single app, model, commerce provider, or communication
channel.

## What Must Remain True

Catch America stays an operator of the platform, never a hard-coded assumption
inside it. Fishing stays a domain pack.

AI is a replaceable reasoning dependency. AI does not own truth, memory,
business rules, or authority.

External providers accelerate the platform. They must not define its domain
model.
